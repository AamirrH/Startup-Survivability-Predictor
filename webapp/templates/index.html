How to interpret the result

The model compares funding, dates, country, and industry patterns. Its historical data ends in 2015; performance on current startups has not been validated. Historical funding may also include information recorded after the outcome.

Unknown inputs are filled using training values. A first industry category is used as a simple representation. This is an exploratory classification tool, not a forecast of survival duration.
